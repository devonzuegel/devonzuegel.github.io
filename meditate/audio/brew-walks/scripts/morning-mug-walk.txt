Welcome to your Morning Mug Walk.

Before we begin, make sure your drink has a secure lid, and choose a route where you can give your full attention to traffic, crossings, and the ground beneath you. Keep your eyes open. Let this practice accompany your walk, while your own judgment keeps you safe.

[[slnc 4000]]

Take a moment at the doorway. Feel the warmth or coolness of the cup in your hand. Notice its weight. Notice the simple fact that you prepared something for yourself.

[[slnc 5000]]

Begin walking at an easy pace. There is nowhere special to get to. Let the first few steps be ordinary. Heel, sole, toes. One foot, then the other.

[[slnc 8000]]

Allow your shoulders to drop a little. Let your free hand hang naturally. Unclench your jaw. Make room around your eyes.

[[slnc 7000]]

Now notice three sounds. Perhaps a bird, a car in the distance, a door closing, or the soft rhythm of your own steps. You do not need to name each one. Let sound arrive, and let it pass.

[[slnc 12000]]

When it is safe to pause, take a sip. Notice the aroma before the taste. Feel the temperature, the texture, and the small movement of swallowing. Then continue walking.

[[slnc 10000]]

For the next stretch of the walk, let your attention widen. Take in the shapes and colors around you. Notice the changing light. See if you can look without searching for anything.

[[slnc 15000]]

Thoughts may be planning the day ahead. That is all right. Each time you notice a plan, silently say, later. Then return to one step, one breath, and the cup in your hand.

[[slnc 12000]]

Feel the air on your face. Notice where your clothing touches your skin. Notice the gentle swing of your arms and the subtle shift of balance from side to side.

[[slnc 15000]]

There is nothing to optimize in these few minutes. You are simply walking, breathing, and receiving the morning.

[[slnc 12000]]

Take another sip whenever you find a safe place. Let it be unhurried. Imagine that this sip marks a small beginning—not a demand, just an opening.

[[slnc 12000]]

As the practice closes, choose one quality you would like to carry into the next part of your day. It might be steadiness, curiosity, patience, or ease. You do not need to force it. Let the word walk beside you.
