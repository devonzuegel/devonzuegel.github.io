Welcome to the Around-the-Block Reset. This is a short practice for the space between one part of your day and the next.

If you brought a drink, use a secure lid. Keep your eyes open and stay attentive to traffic, crossings, steps, and other people.

[[slnc 4000]]

As you begin walking, imagine that the doorway behind you marks a boundary. For these few minutes, you do not have to be inside what came before, or already inside what comes next.

[[slnc 10000]]

Feel the first ten steps. No need to count perfectly. Simply notice the contact and release of each foot.

[[slnc 12000]]

Let out a longer breath. Allow your shoulders to settle as you exhale.

[[slnc 8000]]

Look ahead and find one color that catches your attention. Let your eyes rest there for a moment, then continue.

[[slnc 10000]]

Notice what your mind is carrying. A conversation, a decision, a list, a feeling. You do not need to put it down forever. For one block, try carrying it a little more loosely.

[[slnc 12000]]

If it is safe, pause for a sip. Feel the temperature and taste. Let this small physical sensation interrupt the stream of thought.

[[slnc 10000]]

Walk again. Notice the rhythm: step and shift, step and shift. Your body already knows how to move forward.

[[slnc 15000]]

Ask yourself quietly: what can wait until I return?

[[slnc 12000]]

There may be an answer, or there may not. Either is fine. Let the question create a little space.

[[slnc 10000]]

For the last stretch, lift your gaze and take in the whole scene. Light, movement, weather, buildings, trees, sky. You are one moving part of a larger place.

[[slnc 15000]]

Feel one complete breath as you walk.

[[slnc 7000]]

Then another.

[[slnc 7000]]

As this reset ends, choose the next small thing you will do. Only the next thing. Let the rest of the list remain in the distance.

[[slnc 8000]]

Continue walking safely. The practice is complete.
