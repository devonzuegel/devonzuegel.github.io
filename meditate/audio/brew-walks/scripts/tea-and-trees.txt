Welcome to Tea and Trees, a quiet walking practice for a park, a garden, or any route with a little living green.

Use a cup with a secure lid. Keep your eyes open and let safety come first, especially near roads, bicycles, steps, or uneven ground.

[[slnc 5000]]

Begin by standing still. Feel both feet meet the ground. Notice the cup in your hand and the air around your skin.

[[slnc 7000]]

Start walking slowly enough to sense each step, but naturally enough that you do not have to manage it.

[[slnc 10000]]

Look for the nearest plant or tree. Notice its shape before you name it. Trace the line of a branch, the edge of a leaf, or the way grass bends. Let your gaze be soft.

[[slnc 15000]]

Now listen. Hear the closest sound you can find. Then a sound farther away. Let both be part of the same wide field of attention.

[[slnc 15000]]

If you reach a safe place to pause, take a sip of tea. Notice the fragrance. Notice where warmth appears—in your lips, your mouth, your throat, or the hand holding the cup.

[[slnc 12000]]

Continue walking. See whether the pace of your thoughts can be allowed to differ from the pace of your feet. Thoughts may hurry. Your feet can remain unhurried.

[[slnc 15000]]

Choose one tree or patch of green as you pass. Consider how little effort it seems to make in being itself. For a few steps, release the effort to present, perform, or solve.

[[slnc 16000]]

Feel your body moving as a whole. The feet receive the ground. The spine rises. The breath comes and goes. The arm holding your drink makes tiny adjustments without needing instructions.

[[slnc 16000]]

If your mind wanders, gently return to a simple sequence: green, step, breath. Notice something green. Feel one step. Receive one breath.

[[slnc 10000]]

Take another sip when it is safe. This time, notice the aftertaste and the small silence after swallowing.

[[slnc 12000]]

As you walk, offer a quiet thank-you—to the person who grew the tea, to the water, to the tree or plant in front of you, or simply to yourself for making this pocket of time.

[[slnc 15000]]

Let the final minute belong to your surroundings. No task, no lesson. Walk and look. Walk and listen.

[[slnc 30000]]

Begin to notice where you are going next. Feel the cup in your hand and the ground beneath you. Carry this wider attention with you as the practice ends.
