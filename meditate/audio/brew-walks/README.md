# Brew Walk audio provenance

Added 2026-09-23.

## Permissioned recordings

The app owner confirmed that they hold explicit written permission to download and use these recordings in Tranquila:

- `morning-brew-walk.mp3` — “Morning Brew Walk,” Brew Walks CIC. Source: https://insighttimer.com/brewwalks/guided-meditations/morning-brew-walk
- `around-the-block-brew-walk.mp3` — “Around the Block Brew Walk,” Brew Walks CIC. Source: https://insighttimer.com/brewwalks/guided-meditations/around-the-block-brew-walk
- `mindful-coffee-walk.mp3` — “Mindful Coffee Walk,” Headspace. Source: https://www.headspace.com/content/meditation/mindful-coffee-walk/1097

The written permissions themselves are not stored in this public repository.

## Tranquila originals

The other three MP3 files are original, spoken-only Tranquila practices rendered with Kokoro's `af_heart` synthetic voice at 0.65× speaking speed. The authored `[[slnc N]]` pauses are preserved as silence, with no background music. Their source scripts and reproducible renderer are in `scripts/`, and their synthetic narration is disclosed in the app.
