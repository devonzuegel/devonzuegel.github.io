Install on Your iPhone

Add Tranquila to your Home Screen so it loads instantly and works offline, even after quitting the app.

  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button ⮭︎ at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add

Once installed, any meditations you save will play with no connection. The app shell itself is also cached, so it opens immediately offline.
