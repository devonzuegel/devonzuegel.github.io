Sunshine Tabs - my custom tab manager

November 24, 2026

I’d tried nearly every tab manager I could find and figured this was just a harder design problem than I’d given it credit for.

Then I got a bee in my bonnet and made my own... Just few hours in, it was already more useful to me than any I’ve tried over the years!
