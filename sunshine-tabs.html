Sunshine Tabs

November 24, 2026

I had 255 Chrome tabs open. I’d tried nearly every tab manager I could find and figured this was just a harder design problem than I’d given it credit for. Then I got a bee in my bonnet and made my own. A few hours in, it was already more useful to me than any I’d tried.

Sunshine Tabs shows all my Chrome windows in one place. My favorite thing is filtering to tabs I haven’t touched in 30 days, deciding what to keep, and closing the rest in one batch. I can also move tabs between windows, find duplicates, or park a whole set to reopen later.
