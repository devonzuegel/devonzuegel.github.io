Privacy

QR links

Links at qr.devonzuegel.com redirect to a destination chosen by me. When a link opens, this tool may record the code used, the time and an approximate city, region and country supplied by Cloudflare, and send me an email alert. It does not request GPS access, fingerprint visitors, or store raw IP addresses or full browser user-agent strings in its database.

Location estimates can be inaccurate. Opens are requests, not unique people or confirmed camera scans; shared links, reloads and some automated previews may be counted. A placement note describes where I used a code, not where a visitor is.

I can add private notes to individual link opens for context. Visit-level records, their notes and queued email snapshots are deleted after 90 days, with a short delay while scheduled cleanup runs. Aggregate counts remain. A secret-keyed IP hash rotates daily and is used for short-lived flood protection; stored cooldowns expire within an hour. It is not a visitor identifier. Rate-limited requests may not be counted.

Cloudflare, GitHub (for owner sign-in) and Resend process data to operate the service and have their own logging and retention policies. Alert emails can remain in the recipient’s mailbox after database records expire. Only the owner can access this dashboard and its analytics. The redirect itself requires no login.
